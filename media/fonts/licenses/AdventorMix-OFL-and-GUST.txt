Avant Garde Naowh.ttf holds outlines of two open fonts:
  - TeX Gyre Adventor (Regular, with the digits and the marks . , : ; - of Bold), under the
    GUST Font License, which is based on the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c. That license
    lets a changed font be passed on under another license that keeps its conditions
    (clause 7 of the LPPL): the font carries a name of its own, the changes are listed in
    README.txt beside this file, and the unchanged font is at
    https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/adventor
  - Didact Gothic (its Cyrillic letters), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which asks
    for a font that holds its outlines to be passed on under that license as a whole.
So the file as a whole is passed on under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Both texts follow.
