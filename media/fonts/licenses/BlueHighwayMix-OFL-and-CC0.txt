EUI Highway.ttf, EUI Highway Black.ttf and EUI Highway Caps.ttf hold outlines of two open fonts:
  - Blue Highway Bold, by Raymond Larabie (Typodermic), dedicated to the public domain under
    the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication. The font's own statement:
      "This font has been released under a no rights reserved Creative Commons Zero license.
      [...] To the extent possible under law, Raymond Larabie has waived all copyright and
      related or neighboring rights to this font. This work is published from Japan."
      https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
    The unchanged font is at https://typodermicfonts.com
  - Roboto (ExtraBold for EUI Highway.ttf and EUI Highway Caps.ttf, Black for EUI Highway
    Black.ttf, semi-condensed width): the characters Blue Highway does not have, the Cyrillic
    letters among them, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which asks for a font that holds
    its outlines to be passed on under that license as a whole.
So each file as a whole is passed on under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which follows.
The changes made to both fonts are listed in README.txt beside this file.
