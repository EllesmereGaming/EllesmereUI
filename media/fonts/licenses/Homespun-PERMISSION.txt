The font file is unchanged. Brian Kent's permission to redistribute his fonts, to build new
fonts on them and to change them (changes noted, the originals attributed to him) is on
public record in the copyright file of Debian's fonts-aenigma package:
https://sources.debian.org/src/fonts-aenigma/0.0.20080511+dfsg-5/debian/copyright/

The exchange recorded there (2008-07-04):

  Brian Kent:
    Yes, you can redistribute any of my fonts.

  Debian:
    As I said, it's not enough to give us the permission, it has to extend to all our
    users who need to be able
    - to distribute your fonts further, without asking you
    - to create new fonts, based on your fonts, without asking you
    - to make changes to your fonts, provided those changes are noted and the original
      fonts are still attributed to you.
    Is this what you intended when you wrote the above?

  Brian Kent:
    Hi,
    Yes, all of the things mentioned above I agree to.
